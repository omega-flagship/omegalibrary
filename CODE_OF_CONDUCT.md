# Code of conduct

OmegaLibrary adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, unchanged:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short:
- Be welcoming, respectful and patient. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised content, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, own your mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community.
- Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may warn, suspend or ban participants, for
  behaviour that breaks these standards.

The standards apply in all project spaces and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers' team (the repository owners), not in a public
issue. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence. Enforcement follows the Covenant's community impact
guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
